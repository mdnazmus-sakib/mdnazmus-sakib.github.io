About me

I'm Md. Nazmus Sakib. What started as a handful of studio frames has grown into a body of work that moves between clean portraits, bold colour and compositions built in post.

I care about pictures that have a clear point of view — whether that's a single hard light source, a deep red backdrop, or a poster that carries a name across the frame. If you'd like to work together, I'd be glad to hear from you.
